Content Licenses & Attribution

Worlds built on the 5th Edition or Pathfinder 2nd Edition rulesets can import real, official content alongside anything Chronicled generates. That canonical content comes from free, openly-licensed reference material — never from copyrighted books, paid PDFs, or third-party aggregator sites. This page carries the attribution those licenses require.

5th Edition monster content available for import in Chronicled is sourced from the free System Reference Document 5.1 (“SRD 5.1”), released by Wizards of the Coast LLC under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (“CC-BY-4.0”), via a structured-data conversion by Tabyltop.

Conversion of this document from the original PDF to other formats was performed by the team at Tabyltop.

Spells, classes, and non-monster items are not yet available to import in the 5e ruleset — only Homebrew (AI-generated) content is available for those categories today. Chronicled is not endorsed by, associated with, or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast.

Pathfinder 2nd Edition

A canonical import library for Pathfinder 2e is not available yet. Chronicled only ships officially-licensed content it can verify and attribute correctly — a source confirmed to be released under Paizo's ORC license hasn't been identified yet (see the project's development log for what was checked and ruled out). Pathfinder 2e worlds can still generate original Homebrew content today; Chronicled is not endorsed by, associated with, or sponsored by Paizo Inc.

How this shows up in the Archive

Any entry imported from official content is tagged with its source and license note directly on its dossier page. Reflavored entries (AI-rewritten narrative over unchanged official mechanics) and Homebrew entries (fully AI-generated, validated by this ruleset's real formulas) carry their own tagging too, so it's always clear where an entry's mechanics came from.
