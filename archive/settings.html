AI Features

Turn off to hide AI generation, Fill In, Regenerate, Help Me suggestions, and AI portrait generation. Manual entry, random rolls, and image upload always stay available.

Export

Download the whole world as a PDF -- for reading, printing, or bringing to the table away from a screen. Category- and entry-level exports are also available from each category tab and dossier page.

Delete World

Permanently deletes every NPC, enemy, item, class, log, survivor, and faction this world has generated, along with all portraits and world lore/config. You'll be sent back into a fresh wizard to start over. Your login stays active -- this does not delete your account.

This cannot be undone.
