1. What Chronicled Is

Chronicled is a tool that generates tabletop RPG campaign content — NPCs, monsters, items, factions, locations, and related material — along with AI-generated portrait art, and organizes it into a browsable archive for your own campaign or setting.

2. Plans & Usage

Chronicled is currently in free beta. Every world gets 25 free content generations, no payment method required. There's no subscription or credit purchase active right now — see the pricing & beta status page for the honest current state of this.

A billing system is built for when Chronicled leaves beta, and isn't switched on yet:

  • Subscription — a monthly plan that includes a set number of generations per billing cycle, billed automatically until canceled.
  • Credits — one-time purchases that add extra generations on top of your monthly plan. Credits don't expire and roll over between billing cycles.

Generation limits, pricing, and what counts toward them may change as the product develops, including when billing is switched on. We'll make a reasonable effort to communicate material changes in advance, and beta usage/worlds carry over rather than reset.

3. Billing & Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly. By subscribing or purchasing credits (once that's switched on), you authorize us to charge your payment method through Stripe for the applicable amount on the applicable schedule.

Subscriptions renew automatically each billing cycle until canceled. You'll be able to cancel or update your payment method at any time from the Settings page, which opens Stripe's secure self-serve billing portal. Canceling stops future charges; it doesn't retroactively refund the current cycle.

Purchased credits are non-refundable but do not expire, including if your subscription is later canceled.

Refund requests outside of the above are handled case-by-case — email ausfarr@gmail.com.
