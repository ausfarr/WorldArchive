1. What Chronicled Is

Chronicled is a tool that generates tabletop RPG campaign content — NPCs, monsters, items, factions, locations, and related material — along with AI-generated portrait art, and organizes it into a browsable archive for your own campaign or setting.

2. Plans & Usage

You can try Chronicled with no account at all (a small daily allowance, resets every day, nothing saved). Every signed-up account then gets a free monthly allowance: 10 content generations + 1 image per month, no payment method required, resetting every billing cycle for as long as the account exists. See the pricing page for the current numbers.

Beyond the free account, two ways to get more:

  • Subscription — a $4.99/month plan that includes 50 generations + 10 images per billing cycle, billed automatically until canceled, includes unlimited entries per world, and unlocks regenerating/remixing an entry Chronicled already generated for you.
  • Credits — one-time purchases ($2 per 5 generations) that add extra generations on top of your free account or subscription. Credits don't expire and roll over between billing cycles. Credits cover generations only, not images.

Separately, every world gets 30 free entries (an entry is any NPC, monster, item, faction, or other piece of content in your archive, generated or manually created); beyond that, entry packs ($5 per 25 entries) add more. Active subscribers don't need entry packs — entries are unlimited while subscribed.

Generation and entry limits, pricing, and what counts toward them may change as the product develops. We'll make a reasonable effort to communicate material changes in advance, and existing usage/worlds carry over rather than reset when they do.

3. Billing & Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly. By subscribing or purchasing credits or entry packs, you authorize us to charge your payment method through Stripe for the applicable amount on the applicable schedule.

Subscriptions renew automatically each billing cycle until canceled. You can cancel or update your payment method at any time from the Settings page, which opens Stripe's secure self-serve billing portal. Canceling stops future charges; it doesn't retroactively refund the current cycle.

If your subscription lapses or is canceled, your monthly generation quota drops to zero and your world's entry cap stops being unlimited, but any purchased credits and any entries or worlds you've already created remain exactly as they are — nothing is deleted or locked. Purchased credits and entry packs are non-refundable but do not expire, including after a subscription is canceled.
