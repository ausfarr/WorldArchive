Pricing & beta status

Chronicled is in free beta. Billing is built but switched off — nobody is being charged anything right now. This page is meant to be the honest version of "what does this cost," both today and once it launches.

Right now

You're in free beta

Every world currently gets 25 free generations to work with — no card, no trial clock, no catch. That cap exists to keep beta costs sane while the product is being tested, not as a sales funnel; it isn't tied to a billing system that's currently active. If you run out and want more room to keep testing, email us.

Why publish exact numbers for something that isn't live? Because they're not made up — they're the actual configured price and quota in the billing code, just gated behind a kill switch while we make sure the free beta is a genuinely useful test first. We'd rather show you the real shape than a vague "contact us for pricing."

Will my beta usage or world carry over when billing turns on?

Yes. Turning billing on doesn't reset or delete anything — your world, everything generated in it, and your account stay exactly as they are.

Will the $5/mo price or 25-generation quota change before launch?

Possibly — this is what's built today, not a locked-in promise. If the numbers move before billing turns on, this page gets updated, not quietly changed underneath you.

What happens if I run out of beta generations?

Email ausfarr@gmail.com — during beta, more room to test is a conversation, not a paywall.

One world per account — is that permanent?

That's the current limitation, not a planned permanent restriction. It isn't scoped or scheduled yet, so we're not promising a date here.
