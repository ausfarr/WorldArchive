Why publish exact numbers instead of "contact us for pricing"? Because they're not made up — this page describes the actual configured prices and quotas in the billing system, the same ones you'll see at checkout.

Will my free-account usage or world carry over if I subscribe?

Yes. Subscribing doesn't reset or delete anything — your world, everything generated in it, and your account stay exactly as they are; the monthly quota just gets bigger going forward.

Could the $4.99/mo price or the generation/image numbers change?

Possibly — these are today's real numbers, not a locked-in-forever promise. If anything changes, this page gets updated, not quietly changed underneath you.

What happens if I run out of generations or images this month?

Wait for next month's reset, subscribe for a bigger monthly quota, or buy a credit pack — see the plans above. Questions first? Email ausfarr@gmail.com.

Why can't free accounts regenerate/remix an entry?

Fresh generation, filling in a placeholder, Manual Mode edits, and everything else stay free either way — regenerating an entry Chronicled already wrote for you specifically is the one thing reserved for subscribers.

One world per account — is that permanent?

That's the current limitation, not a planned permanent restriction. It isn't scoped or scheduled yet, so we're not promising a date here.
