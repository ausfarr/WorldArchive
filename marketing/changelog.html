Build Log

What's new in Chronicled — new generators, features, and fixes, as they ship. Written for anyone following along, not just for the dev team.

● Currently in beta  ·  Latest version: v0.9

Up next

Archive search + category page grouping/ordering

Scoped and confirmed, not yet built — search across your whole archive, plus better grouping and ordering on category pages.

v0.9[08/10/2026]

Manual Mode

  • Build any entry by hand, start to finish — no AI required. Same editing forms as before, just starting from a blank entry instead of a generated one.
  • New "Roll Randomly" option on every category — an instant, free entry pulled together from weighted tables instead of a live AI call. It reads your world's genre and reskins itself accordingly, so a fantasy world rolls enchanted blades and cursed ruins while a post-apocalyptic world rolls scrap-fused scavenger gear, not a one-size-fits-all mashup.
  • The "+ Create Entry" button is now one clean menu — Generate with AI, Enter Manually, or Roll Randomly — instead of three separate buttons competing for space.
  • New "Help me" button on individual fields — get a suggestion for just the one thing you're stuck on, instead of regenerating a whole entry.
  • New account-level "AI Features" switch in Settings — turn AI off entirely and Manual Entry + Roll Randomly keep working with zero risk of an accidental AI call.
  • World setup is now fully optional on AI, top to bottom — every step already let you type your own answers, and the two spots that used to generate things automatically (world art, and expanding factions into full lore) now ask first.
  • Fixed leftover placeholder text showing up on pages where you'd left an optional field blank.
v0.8[08/03/2026]

Quests, Campaigns & Battle Maps

  • Build Quests out of your NPCs, Locations, Enemies, Items, and Logs — put one together by hand, or let Chronicled propose one from what you've already created.
  • String multiple Quests together into a Campaign — a full story arc, planned and sequenced for you.
  • Generate a battle map for any Location, grid included — right-click to save it and use it at the table or in your favorite VTT.
  • Export a Quest or Campaign as a print-ready PDF, with every NPC, Location, and stat block it references bundled right in.
  • Enemies can now be part of a Quest, so your encounters actually include what you're fighting.
  • Signature lines and quotes across NPCs, factions, classes, and enemies should feel less like AI filler and more like something a character would actually say.
  • Assorted reliability improvements and bug fixes.
v0.7[07/30/2026]

Locations & Maps

  • A new content category: generate Locations for your world, complete with a controlling faction, notable features, and any NPCs tied to that place.
  • Every world now gets a real generated map, automatically laid out from your locations — not just a flavor image.
v0.6[07/28/2026]

Chronicled is here

  • Chronicled name created, with a new home at chronicled.world.
  • Usage limits and a Settings page added for beta — see your generation usage at a glance.
v0.5[07/27/2026]

A new look for every world, and skills & stats your way

  • Each world now gets its own visual identity — pick a palette and style during setup, and every page, including each faction's accent color, reflects it.
  • Portrait art generation now matches your world's chosen style automatically, instead of one fixed look for everyone.
  • Give your world its own name and tagline — the whole site now reflects it, not just the content inside it.
  • Rename the six core stats and weapon skills to fit your world's own vocabulary — the numbers underneath stay balanced, only the labels change.
  • Added a permanent World Info page — a living reference sheet for your world's identity, lore, and stats, always up to date.
v0.4[07/25/2026]

Every generator now speaks your world's language

  • NPCs, enemies, items, classes, logs, survivors, and factions are now all generated grounded in your world's lore and factions — not a fixed template.
  • Fixed a bug affecting portrait generation for some content types.
v0.3[07/23/2026]

Faster, more connected archive pages

  • Archive pages now stay in sync with what you've actually generated, instead of needing a manual refresh step.
  • Factions created during setup now show up immediately in your live archive.
v0.2[07/22/2026]

Guided world setup

  • New worlds now start with a step-by-step setup wizard — lore, factions, stats, style, and category preferences, with a "generate for me" option on nearly every field.
  • Import an existing world bible/lore document, or generate one from scratch.
  • Progress now saves automatically as you go through setup, so closing the tab won't lose your work.
v0.1[07/20/2026]

Accounts & private worlds

  • Chronicled now supports individual accounts — your worlds are private to you.
