## Long description (~600–800 chars, for directories with a bigger field)

Most AI worldbuilding tools are good at inventing new things and bad at
remembering what they already invented — a new NPC contradicts a faction
you made last week, a monster doesn't fit the tone you set up. Chronicled
fixes that by grounding every generation in your world's own established
lore, factions, and stat system, so new content stays consistent with what
already exists instead of drifting. A guided setup wizard captures your
world once (lore, factions, a custom stat system, a visual style guide),
then generation for NPCs, monsters, items, factions, and locations pulls
from that same context every time. Everything files automatically into a
private, per-user browsable archive — no manual wiki-building. Free beta,
10 generations with no card required.
